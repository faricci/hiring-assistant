# Team Profile — DevOps / Platform Engineering

> One open position, **two contribution archetypes**. P1 and P2 are not separate
> roles or vacancies — they are two operating prevalences within the same position.
> A candidate can sit anywhere on the spectrum; the routing only helps you decide
> where they generate the most value.

## Hiring philosophy

- We hire engineers who give a **real contribution** to current and future projects,
  not "configuration monkeys". We want people who reason, design and improve systems.
- **Dev → Delivery, not Ops → DevOps**. The ideal candidate has a software
  development background and chose to move toward delivery/platform. This ensures
  they understand developers and can improve software processes from the inside.
  An ops person who added DevOps keywords to their CV is the profile to avoid.
- A candidate who knows everything but has a problematic attitude is **worse** than
  a slightly less technical one with the right mindset.
- If a candidate is not brilliant: **flag it**, do not auto-reject. But the goal is
  to find genuinely strong people.
- The new hire joins a small team with cross-project responsibilities: they must be
  **autonomous** and **proactive**.

## Archetype P1 — Platform / Application Engineering

**Profile**: developer with a full-stack background who builds internal platforms.

### Key accountabilities
- Design and build internal platforms: SDLC monitoring, developer-experience
  platforms, self-service dashboards.
- Build self-service tools for onboarding, monitoring and workflow automation.
- Define architecture and design of internal applications.
- Integrate with existing systems (issue tracking, CI, cloud, observability, VCS).
- Drive technical decisions on stack, architectural patterns and API design.

### Must-have skills
- **Background**: full-stack developer, 3-5+ years building applications/platforms.
- **Backend**: Python (FastAPI/Flask/Django) OR Node.js/TypeScript — able to design APIs and services.
- **Frontend**: React OR Angular OR Vue — able to build functional UIs.
- **Database**: PostgreSQL or equivalent — schema design, queries, migrations.
- **Containers**: Docker — able to containerize their own applications.
- **CI/CD**: GitHub Actions or GitLab CI — designs the workflow, not just uses it.
- **API design**: RESTful, ideally event-driven.
- **Version control**: advanced Git.
- **Mindset**: engineering mindset, end-to-end ownership, continuous improvement.
- **English**: working proficiency.

### Nice-to-have skills
- AI/LLM/RAG experience — strong plus for intelligent platforms.
- Kubernetes / container orchestration.
- Cloud services (ECS, Lambda, S3, managed observability) for platform deployment.
- Monitoring/observability (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry).
- Terraform to manage the platforms' own infrastructure.
- Internal developer platforms (Backstage, Port, custom).
- Event-driven architecture (Kafka, queues, webhooks).
- Dashboard design and data visualization.

## Archetype P2 — Delivery / DevOps Engineering

**Profile**: DevOps/SRE engineer (senior or junior depending on budget) supporting
daily operations and delivery.

### Key accountabilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines for complex applications.
- Manage infrastructure as code (Terraform, Ansible) across multi-tier environments.
- Manage and optimize cloud infrastructure (governance, cost, security, migration).
- Containerize and orchestrate services (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Support and improve monitoring and observability platforms.
- Contribute to SDLC standards and DevOps culture: automation, continuous
  improvement, blameless culture.

### Must-have skills
- **Experience**: 5+ years in DevOps/SRE/Platform Engineering (senior) OR 2-3 years
  with strong potential (junior).
- **CI/CD**: concrete experience with Jenkins, GitHub Actions or GitLab CI — pipeline
  design, not just usage.
- **IaC**: Terraform OR Ansible (ideally both) — production infrastructure.
- **Containers**: Docker (must), orchestration concepts.
- **Cloud**: concrete production experience (not just certification).
- **Linux**: RHEL/CentOS administration, Bash scripting.
- **Scripting**: Python for automation and tooling.
- **Version control**: advanced Git (branching strategies, merge conflicts, hooks).
- **Networking**: basic understanding (DNS, load balancing, firewalls, VPN).
- **Mindset**: continuous improvement, curiosity, constant learning, energy.
- **English**: working proficiency.

### Nice-to-have skills
- **Kubernetes** in production (strong nice-to-have, future direction).
- Monitoring stack (Prometheus, Grafana, ELK/OpenSearch, OpenTelemetry).
- Build systems (Conan, CMake, RPM packaging).
- Advanced configuration management (Ansible roles/collections).
- Infrastructure security (firewall rules, certificate/secrets management).
- Microservices and orchestration (ECS, EKS).
- Incident management and on-call experience.
- Experience in a regulated / financial domain.

## Certifications (secondary indicator)

Count as a **real plus** only when demanding:
- Cloud Professional-level (Solutions Architect Professional, DevOps Engineer Professional)
- CKA (Certified Kubernetes Administrator)
- HashiCorp Terraform Associate (minor, but signals formalization)

Easy entry-level certifications add no evaluation value.

## Cross-cutting competencies (non-negotiable)

- English working proficiency.
- Problem solving: decompose complex problems into manageable steps.
- Ownership: carry a task forward without micromanagement.
- Teamwork: real collaboration, not just "working next to".
- Learning agility: pick up new tools/technologies quickly.
- Proactivity: propose improvements without being asked.

## Evaluation framework — Behaviour → Skills → Knowledge

| # | Dimension | Weight | Evaluates | Minimum |
|---|-----------|--------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | **Behaviour** | 40% | attitude, energy, curiosity, honesty, communication, teamwork | < 3.0 requires an evidence review |
| 2 | **Skills** | 35% | problem solving, abstraction, adaptability, ownership, technical leadership | >= 2.5 |
| 3 | **Knowledge** | 25% | technical depth, breadth, currency, AI awareness | >= 2.5 |

### 1-5 scale

| Score | Label | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| 5 | Excellent | Exceeds expectations; raises the team's level. |
| 4 | Good | Fully meets; solid, reliable, real contribution. |
| 3 | Sufficient | Acceptable but not brilliant. |
| 2 | Insufficient | Significant gaps; too much investment needed. |
| 1 | Not suitable | Fundamental misalignment. |

### Provisional recommendation thresholds

| Weighted score | Signal |
|----------------|--------|
| >= 4.0 | Strong provisional recommendation |
| 3.5 - 3.9 | Provisional recommendation — monitor weak areas |
| 3.0 - 3.4 | Not brilliant — flag, do not auto-reject |
| < 3.0 | Provisional proposal not to proceed — validate evidence |
| Behaviour < 3.0 | Mandatory evidence and bias review |

> **Promising juniors exception**: a junior with strong potential signals
> (demanding certifications, side projects, learning speed, energy) is worth
> interviewing even below the seniority target. Round 1 focuses on mindset,
> learning speed, curiosity and ownership potential; technical knowledge weighs less.

## Red flags

### Behavioural (deepen in evidence review)
- **Arrogance**: talks over people, does not listen, dismisses questions.
- **Dishonesty**: describes experiences they did not have (inconsistencies when probed).
- **Zero curiosity**: "I don't follow blogs/communities, I do what I'm told".
- **Blame culture**: always blames colleagues, management or tools — never owns.
- **Passive attitude**: waits for instructions, never proposed improvements.

### CV
- Buzzwords without substance: a long tool list with no depth on any.
- Unexplained timeline gaps with no reasonable motivation.
- Inflated titles: "Lead DevOps Architect" with 2 years of experience.
- No mention of collaboration, only "I did X, I built Y".
- Certifications as the sole competence indicator, no real projects.

### Warnings (monitor, not eliminatory)
- Experience concentrated on a single stack/vendor → verify adaptability.
- No experience in distributed teams → the role needs remote collaboration.
- Only small-startup experience → verify ability to operate in a structured org (and vice versa).
